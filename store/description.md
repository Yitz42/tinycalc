# TinySci: store listing

**Name:** TinySci
**Category:** Tools & Utilities
**Platform:** Pebble Time 2 (emery)

## Short description (one line)

A touch calculator that types like math: exact answers, an equation solver, systems, calculus, graphs and tools.

## Full description

TinySci is a scientific calculator built for the Pebble Time 2's touch screen.

**Math that looks like math.** What you type is shown as real math as you go: fractions stack, powers rise, and √, π and × are drawn properly. Hold on the display to get a cursor and edit anywhere.

**Solve equations with the = key.** Type an equation in x, like x³ − 2x = 1, and press =. No starting guess needed: it finds the roots in view and widens the search when there are none. When an equation is really a polynomial, you also get exact answers like (1 − √5)/2, and the equation simplified. Put a y in it and it asks for a second equation, then solves the pair.

**Calculus, as numbers or formulas.** ∫dx gives a definite integral and d/dx a derivative at a point, or leave the limits empty for the formula: d/dx x²·sin x = 2x·sin x + x²·cos x, ∫ x·sin x dx = sin x − x·cos x + C. **Factor** and **Expand** work on polynomials: x² − x − 6 = (x + 2)(x − 3).

**Graphing.** Graph y = f(x), both sides of an equation, or curves in x and y like x² + y² = 25. Drag, zoom, and tap to read off points.

**History.** Your last 8 answers are saved. Tap one to bring it back and keep calculating with it.

**Your way.** In the app's settings in the Pebble app on your phone, you can choose:
- what each button does, pressed and held, from a list of actions
- graphs on the solver sheet
- key buzz strength
- how the backlight behaves

**Everything else:** sin, cos, tan and sinh, cosh, tanh with their inverses, in degrees or radians. nCr, nPr, mod, gcd, lcm, round and floor. Also ln, log, eˣ, 10ˣ, √, ∛, x², x³, xʸ, n!, |x|, %, 1/x, EE and Ans. STO and RCL store and recall x, and the formula view shows exact forms like √12 = 2√3.

## How to use it

- Swipe left or right to switch between the number keys and the function keys. Swipe up for the solver keys.
- **Select** is =, **Back** deletes, **Down** clears, and **Up** changes the page.
- Hold **Up** for History (it's also on the solver keys).
- Settings are in the Pebble app on your phone: open TinySci and tap its settings.
- Pull down from the display, or hold **Down**, for the formula view.
